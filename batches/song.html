ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Singing Stars is a comprehensive music education program designed to develop talented singers and musicians through professional vocal training, instrumental learning, music theory, stage performance, and creative expression.

Students learn Indian, Western and Contemporary music, play various instruments, understand rhythm, compose melodies and perform live with confidence.

"More than a singing class, it's a complete journey of music, creativity, confidence and stage excellence."
