ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Yoga Awakening is a unique Sunday special program that combines Yoga, Meditation and Spiritual Wisdom to build students physically strong, mentally calm and morally upright.

"A journey towards a healthy body, peaceful mind and a noble character."

🌿

🌿

🧠

Develop Focus, Discipline and Self-Control

🛡️

Strengthen Moral Values and Character

🕉️

Introduce Yogic Wisdom and Indian Spirituality

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Create Responsible, Confident and Compassionate Leaders
