ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Startup Spark is a comprehensive entrepreneurship and innovation program specially designed for students who dream of becoming entrepreneurs, business leaders, innovators, and changemakers.

This practical, project-based learning program introduces students to the exciting world of startups, business development, innovation, finance, leadership, technology, marketing, and problem-solving.

Students will learn how successful companies are built, how to identify business opportunities, validate ideas, manage money, build teams, and confidently present their ideas. The program combines real-world business knowledge with hands-on activities, startup simulations, group projects, case studies, and mentorship.

"More than just a business class, Startup Spark is a journey of innovation, leadership, creativity, financial intelligence, and building the future."
